\label{sec:retired-assumptions}

geometry into five numbered assumptions A1--A5.  That register is retained
here only as a historical crosswalk.  The controlling interface of the present paper is
Hypotheses~\ref{hyp:P0}--\ref{hyp:P3} together with the uninhabited Lean
fields \texttt{ExternalGeometry} and \texttt{CSExternalGeometry}.  None of
A1--A5 is proved as stated, and none is used as a premise of
Theorem~\ref{thm:joined}.

\paragraph{What A1--A5 asked.}
A1 required the frozen framed diagram to be the actual reduced handle
presentation of $\Sigma_A^0$, with one common framed movie transporting
collar, detector insertion and point-push chronology.  A2 required the MWW
one-handle hypotheses, endpoint shadow $u=e_2-e_{87}$, whole-module
filtration, and the four grading contributions $-44,+227,+315,-4$.  A3
required the statewise divided-cubic beta/psi cocone on every finite cable
state.  A4 required embedded chosen spheres satisfying Horvat--Jab\l onowski
E7 and MWW three-handle formulas, with leading Frobenius form on the whole
source.  A5 required rational graded closure through the four-handle and a
vanishing degree-$494$ piece for $S^4$.

\paragraph{Why the register is not deleted.}
The old wording remains the most explicit statement of what would have been
required to close the historical-PD route.  Removing it would erase the
claim boundary between finite certificates and geometric identification.
Independent review of the retired route should still treat A1 as falsified
for the unavailable planar diagram and A4 as the last load-bearing point of
that manuscript; the Johnson replacement argument above supersedes that route
without proving the old assumption statements verbatim.

\paragraph{How each old assumption could still be attacked.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{A1.}  Give a framed Kirby movie from the standard
Cappell--Shaneson surgery to the frozen cut presentation, transporting every
label, normal field, basepoint and detector insertion disk.  The Johnson
certificate replaces this for $X_J$, not for the historical PD.
\item \textbf{A2.}  Construct the actual coefficient equivalence from paired
annuli and prove both action-naturality squares.  Lemma~\ref{lem:C1} does
this for the Johnson replacement collar only.
\item \textbf{A3.}  Prove the beta/psi equations on every finite cable state
as equalities of linear maps on their full source spaces.  Equations
(26)--(27) do this after C1.
\item \textbf{A4.}  Embed the finite sphere models in one actual
$\partial W_2$, verify E7, and identify whole-source MWW maps with the
leading Frobenius form.  The reversed belt-sphere proof of S replaces the
TH1/TH2/THXY combinatorial models.
\item \textbf{A5.}  Match published coefficient and grading conventions and
check bidegree $(0,0)$ for the four-handle isomorphism.  E12 separates the
computed ranks from the cited MWW isomorphisms.
\end{itemize}

\begin{remark}[No inference from filenames or certificates]
Names such as ``global descending certificate'', ``chosen sphere receipt'',
or ``Hattori decision'' are locators for data.  They do not occur as
premises of any proof in this article.  Whenever a finite file is used, the
relevant mathematical content is stated explicitly and the replay command is
given in Section~\ref{sec:reproducibility-extended}.
\end{remark}
