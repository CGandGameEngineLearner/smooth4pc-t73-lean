# Independent review and reproducibility note

## Status

This repository contains a **candidate proof pending independent external
review** for the Cappell--Shaneson manifold denoted (X(41,189,73)).  It is
not a peer-accepted counterexample to the smooth four-dimensional Poincare
conjecture.  The strongest checked Lean result is conditional: finite algebra
and quotient arguments are kernel-checked.  The current paper supplies
paper-level proofs of P0a--c, C1--C2 and the essential-sphere endpoint
comparison; these geometric lemmas remain outside Lean and require
independent mathematical review.  The exact
conditional endpoint is in
[`Smooth4PC/T73Conditional.lean`](../Smooth4PC/T73Conditional.lean), and the
current mathematical allocation is in the LaTeX paper and the research notes
under [`docs/research/`](research/).

## The three-step argument

### 1. Construct and detect a degree-494 class

The proposed class begins in a raw one-handle state.  A coefficient trace and
quantum-Hochschild shadow are used to define a divided cubic detector; on the
selected class the claimed value is

\[
(-2)^3(-328)=2624\ne0,
\qquad q=-44+227+315-4=494.
\]

Lean recomputes these two equalities from their primitive integer inputs in
[`Smooth4PC/T73Finite.lean`](../Smooth4PC/T73Finite.lean).  The coefficient
coend, cyclic quotient and naturality algebra are formalized in
[`Smooth4PC/CoefficientTrace.lean`](../Smooth4PC/CoefficientTrace.lean), while
the balanced input model is separated in
[`Smooth4PC/HattoriBalancedInput.lean`](../Smooth4PC/HattoriBalancedInput.lean).
The mathematical and computational bindings are recorded in
[`T73_EVIDENCE_QTRACE_SOURCE_LEDGER.md`](proofs/T73_EVIDENCE_QTRACE_SOURCE_LEDGER.md),
[`T73_EVIDENCE_RAW_STATE_BINDING.md`](proofs/T73_EVIDENCE_RAW_STATE_BINDING.md)
and
[`T73_EVIDENCE_ETA_T1_DELTA3.md`](proofs/T73_EVIDENCE_ETA_T1_DELTA3.md).
The frozen input certificate is checked and regenerated by
[`scripts/check_certificate_sha.py`](../scripts/check_certificate_sha.py),
[`scripts/check_generated_data.py`](../scripts/check_generated_data.py) and
[`scripts/generate_certificate_data.py`](../scripts/generate_certificate_data.py).

What Lean proves here is the integer arithmetic and the abstract trace/quotient
logic.  The assertion that the named raw state and detector are the actual
geometric (X(41,189,73)) objects is part of the candidate proof and still
requires independent mathematical review; see the scope statement in
[`T73_COUNTEREXAMPLE_MATERIALS_INDEX.md`](proofs/T73_COUNTEREXAMPLE_MATERIALS_INDEX.md).

### 2. Descend through the two- and three-handle relations

The detector must annihilate the complete MWW beta/psi relations and the six
relations supplied by the three chosen spheres.  The general quotient algebra
is implemented in
[`Smooth4PC/T73SphereQuotient.lean`](../Smooth4PC/T73SphereQuotient.lean),
[`Smooth4PC/T73SplitMovie.lean`](../Smooth4PC/T73SplitMovie.lean) and
[`Smooth4PC/QuotientEquiv.lean`](../Smooth4PC/QuotientEquiv.lean).  The
candidate-specific beta/psi and chosen-sphere evidence is documented in
[`T73_EVIDENCE_ONE_CUP_E5_E6.md`](proofs/T73_EVIDENCE_ONE_CUP_E5_E6.md) and
[`T73_EVIDENCE_E8_CHOSEN_SPHERES.md`](proofs/T73_EVIDENCE_E8_CHOSEN_SPHERES.md).
The actual-handle-presentation bridge is stated in
[`T73_GA1_DESCENDING_BRIDGE.md`](proofs/T73_GA1_DESCENDING_BRIDGE.md), with an
independent replay of its finite descending check in
[`scripts/verify_t73_global_descending.py`](../scripts/verify_t73_global_descending.py).

The formal files prove: if the displayed maps are the actual MWW maps, if the
chosen spheres satisfy the stated geometric bindings, and if the complete MWW
quotient theorem applies, then the functional descends and the selected class
remains nonzero.  They do not construct an inhabitant of those actual-geometry
interfaces; the interface boundary is visible in
[`Smooth4PC/Interfaces.lean`](../Smooth4PC/Interfaces.lean) and
[`Smooth4PC/T73External.lean`](../Smooth4PC/T73External.lean).

### 3. Identify the closed manifold and compare it with (S^4)

The last step uses the four-handle isomorphism, the degree-zero support of the
standard four-sphere module, graded diffeomorphism invariance, and the
Cappell--Shaneson topology calculation.  Lean isolates the finite lattice
calculations in
[`Smooth4PC/T73CSAlgebra.lean`](../Smooth4PC/T73CSAlgebra.lean), the external
topology implications in
[`Smooth4PC/T73CSTopology.lean`](../Smooth4PC/T73CSTopology.lean), and the
(B^4\to S^4) support reduction in
[`Smooth4PC/T73S4Control.lean`](../Smooth4PC/T73S4Control.lean).  Thus the
kernel checks the implication from these hypotheses to “homotopy sphere and
not diffeomorphic to (S^4)”; it does not replace the hypotheses with a
formalized theory of smooth four-manifolds.

## Independent delta recomputation

The exact word calculation was independently reproduced using integer
arithmetic in the truncated polynomial ring.  The checked profile begins

```text
[0, 0, 0, -328, 14596, -410246, 9595271]
```

for (\ell(\rho(W)-I)u) in the epsilon coordinate, giving
([h^3]=-8\cdot(-328)=2624); the same audit found the squared shell begins only
at order six.  The committed evidence, input identities and recomputation
hashes are in
[`T73_EVIDENCE_ETA_T1_DELTA3.md`](proofs/T73_EVIDENCE_ETA_T1_DELTA3.md).
Lean independently checks only the final substitution arithmetic in
[`Smooth4PC/T73Finite.lean`](../Smooth4PC/T73Finite.lean); it does not execute
the 45,360-letter braid calculation.

A compact public recomputation is now included as
[`scripts/recompute_t73_delta3.py`](../scripts/recompute_t73_delta3.py), with
primitive input rows in
[`data/T73_DELTA3_PUBLIC_INPUT.json`](../data/T73_DELTA3_PUBLIC_INPUT.json).
Run it with:

```text
python -I -B scripts/recompute_t73_delta3.py --check
```

The input contains neither the expanded 11,340-/45,360-letter words nor the
integers `-328` and `2624`.  The script reconstructs both words, checks their
hashes and computes the truncated polynomial profile before printing
`DELTA3_ETA_T1=2624`.

## Convention-legality audit

The legality rules were frozen in commit `cf4a990` before the detailed L2/L3
search outputs were opened.  The frozen rules are
[`QSTAR_R7_LEGALITY_CRITERIA_PRESEARCH_20260901.md`](proofs/QSTAR_R7_LEGALITY_CRITERIA_PRESEARCH_20260901.md).
The later adjudication is
[`QSTAR_R7_LEGALITY_ADJUDICATION_20260901.md`](proofs/QSTAR_R7_LEGALITY_ADJUDICATION_20260901.md)
in commit `9d75dcd`.

For L2, the registered point-push chronology is legal and gives first anomaly
3 and value 2624.  With the corrected collar endpoints, the alternative
source-sweep ordering has zero cubic coefficient and first anomaly at order
4; it reverses non-disjoint events and is not a legal serialization.  For the
22 enumerated L3 outputs, the result was `LEGAL=0`, `ILLEGAL=19`,
`NOT ESTABLISHED=3`.  None is a verified legal standard-member nonzero collar,
but this does **not** prove that no legal standard collar exists.  Membership
in (\Gamma_3) was treated as an independent antecedent rather than as a
legality rule; the three unestablished candidates also fail to supply that
antecedent in their recorded form.  The detailed row-by-row reasons and the
dirty-environment disclosure are in the adjudication file.

## Review boundary

The repository therefore supports the following statement only:

> Under the explicitly listed geometric identifications and published
> topology/handle theorems, the kernel-checked algebra produces a nonzero
> degree-494 class for the candidate and excludes a diffeomorphism with the
> standard (S^4); the separate Cappell--Shaneson inputs give the conditional
> homotopy-sphere conclusion.

The project does not claim that Lean has instantiated those geometric
interfaces, that the candidate proof has passed independent peer review, or
that a counterexample has been accepted by the mathematical community.  A
reviewer should begin with
[`T73_COUNTEREXAMPLE_MATERIALS_INDEX.md`](proofs/T73_COUNTEREXAMPLE_MATERIALS_INDEX.md),
then audit the three steps above against the original papers and the named
repository evidence.
