# P0/E13 paper-proof attempt and exact obstruction

Date: 2026-09-04  
Scope: P0, the Johnson--Aitchison--Rubinstein geometric bridge, the two
framed (1/2)-handle cancellations, the detector collar, P3, and E13.  This
note deliberately does not use a `PASS` string, a digest, or a stored Boolean
as evidence for a topological assertion.

## Result of the attempt

The current repository is insufficient to prove P0 or E13.  The first missing
datum in logical order is a globally defined relative PL homeomorphism

\[
 \psi_A:(T^3,H_0,H_1,B)\longrightarrow(T^3,H_0,H_1,B)
\]

whose induced homology map is (A).  The repository contains substantial
local combinatorics for a proposed hierarchy, but its final `ArmRestore`
assembly is not a map: it is a list of layer names and cell counts followed by
asserted semantic Booleans.  Consequently there is no object on which one can
check the required global coverage, common-face compatibility, injectivity,
surjectivity, periodic gluing, or inverse identities.  The repository itself
confirms this: its compositor refuses to evaluate the hierarchical map at a
general point and records the general point evaluator as `OPEN`.

Even if that first gap were filled, the two cancellation schedules do not yet
certify embedded framed Kirby bands in a common ambient boundary, the detector
ball is not embedded into a constructed post-cancellation boundary, and P3/E13
do not construct the post-three-handle (S^3) or a four-handle attaching map.
Thus the following argument is a *conditional theorem chain plus a precise
inventory of missing data*, not a proof of (X_J\cong\Sigma_A^0).

## 1. A paper-level lemma which can be proved now

### Lemma 1 (the affine Johnson--AR Heegaard-pair bridge)

Put

\[
 T_J=\mathbb R^3/\mathbb Z^3,\qquad
 T_{AR}=\mathbb R^3/(2\mathbb Z)^3,
\]

and let (K_0,K_1\subset T_J) be Johnson's coordinate roses based at
(0) and ((1/2,1/2,1/2)).  Let (L_B,L_D\subset T_{AR}) be the coordinate
roses based at
(Q=(-1/2,-1/2,-1/2)) and
(\bar Q=(1/2,1/2,1/2)).  Then

\[
 S([u])=[2u-(1/2,1/2,1/2)]
\]

is an orientation-preserving affine diffeomorphism taking
((K_0,K_1)) to ((L_B,L_D)).  It also takes the Euclidean Voronoi
Heegaard pair of (K_0,K_1) to the Euclidean Voronoi pair of (L_B,L_D).

#### Proof

For (n\in\mathbb Z^3), (S(u+n)-S(u)=2n\in(2\mathbb Z)^3), so (S) is
well defined.  Its inverse is
([v]\mapsto[(v+(1/2,1/2,1/2))/2]); hence it is bijective and smooth.
The derivative is (2I), so its determinant is (8>0).  Coordinate lines
through (0) go to coordinate lines through (Q), and coordinate lines
through ((1/2,1/2,1/2)) go to coordinate lines through (\bar Q).
Finally (S) multiplies every torus distance by (2).  Therefore the two
distance inequalities defining Johnson's Voronoi handlebodies are carried
to the corresponding inequalities for (L_B,L_D).  Johnson proves that
the former are regular neighborhoods of the two roses; their images are
therefore valid regular-neighborhood handlebodies of the kind allowed in
the AR construction.  This proves the claim.  □

This proof is stronger and cleaner than the current finite barycenter
assignment.  In particular, no recognition program is needed.  The finite
scripts correctly verify the lattice and spine formulas, but
`certify_t73_spine_star_handlebodies.py` explicitly says that its Euclidean
Voronoi surface is not the recorded subcomplex and leaves
`mapping_torus_handlebody_identification` open.  The paper should use the
similarity proof above, not infer the result from the discrete owner table.

If (B_J) is a sufficiently small ball about (0), then (S(B_J)) is the
ball of twice the radius about (Q).  Thus the same lemma is relative to a
chosen section neighborhood once a monodromy representative is actually
proved to fix (B_J) pointwise.

## 2. What Johnson and AR actually supply

Johnson's Theorem 1 states that the mapping class group of the genus-three
splitting is generated by six (\alpha_{ij}) together with (\sigma,\tau).
Section 2 describes (\alpha_{ij}) by sending one coordinate edge to a
diagonal and isotoping the diagonal to a two-edge path near the spine.  This
proves the existence of splitting-preserving *mapping classes* inducing the
six elementary transvections.  It does not provide the repository's rational
cell hierarchy, a representative fixed on the selected ball, or an ambient
isotopy carrying all labelled attaching curves and normal fields.

Aitchison--Rubinstein, pp. 5--8, supply the relevant handle recipe once a
genuine splitting-preserving diffeomorphism has been chosen.  Their mapping
two-handle attaching circles are made from the cut spine arcs in the bottom
and top fibers and two arcs through the mapping handle; the framing annuli are
constructed at the same time.  Their cancellation argument uses an attaching
sphere meeting a belt sphere once *geometrically*, after all other passages
have been slid off it.  Lemma 3.1 constructs a symmetric representative for
their special CS matrices, and Lemma 3.2 straightens it at the fixed point.
These results validate the intended paper argument after the candidate's
ambient maps and framed bands exist; they do not certify those candidate data.

Primary sources checked:

- Jesse Johnson, *Automorphisms of the three-torus preserving a genus-three
  Heegaard splitting*, Theorem 1 and Sections 2--3:
  <https://msp.org/pjm/2011/253-1/pjm-v253-n1-p05-p.pdf>.
- I. R. Aitchison and J. H. Rubinstein, *Fibered knots and involutions on
  homotopy spheres*, pp. 5--17, Lemmas 3.1--3.2:
  <https://math.berkeley.edu/~kirby/papers/Gordon%20and%20Kirby%20%28editors%29%20-%20Four-manifold%20theory%20%28Durham%29%20-%20MR0780574.pdf>.
- R. Kirby, *A calculus for framed links in (S^3)*:
  <https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01406222>.
- F. Laudenbach and V. Poenaru, *A note on 4-dimensional handlebodies*,
  Theorem A/A': <https://doi.org/10.24033/bsmf.1741>.

No defect in these published results was found.  The failure is in verifying
their hypotheses for the repository's candidate.

## 3. The first exact missing datum: the global relative PL map

For a finite PL certificate to prove that a prescribed piecewise-affine map
(f:K\to K) is a PL homeomorphism, it is enough (after a common subdivision)
to provide:

1. every top-dimensional source simplex and its affine vertex image;
2. equality of the affine formulas on every shared face, including quotient
   faces paired by the period lattice;
3. a target triangulation whose top simplices are covered once, equivalently
   a simplicial inverse with both composites checked on all vertices;
4. a subcomplex description of (H_0,H_1,B), with exact image/preimage
   equality for each subcomplex.

Positive determinants on individual tetrahedra do not replace items 1--3.
They prove only local orientation preservation where a map has already been
defined.

The repository does not provide those data for `ArmRestore`:

- `scripts/build_t73_johnson_restore_assembly.py:118-135` creates two arrays
  of *strings* naming seven layers and their putative inverses.
- At lines 136--152 it records counts and then directly assigns
  `maps_affine_image_handlebody_to_target`, `maps_both_owners_setwise`,
  `fixes_protected_ball_pointwise`, `restore_isotopic_to_identity`, and
  `explicit_inverse` the value `True`.
- The function never evaluates the proposed composition, constructs a common
  refinement of the layer triangulations, verifies that the codomain of one
  layer is the domain of the next, or checks either composite with the named
  inverse.
- This is especially visible because some input layers still say the final
  assembly is open: `build_t73_johnson_cap_collapse_assembly.py` emits
  `paired_saddle_ambient_cells: OPEN` and
  `johnson_restore_ambient_cells: OPEN`; the outer-collar builder emits
  `final_restore_assembly: OPEN`.  The final assembly does not resolve these
  fields by constructing a map; it only ignores them and creates new `PASS`
  fields.
- `scripts/build_t73_johnson_pl_generators.py:77-104` hard-codes the complete
  Heegaard owner matrix and the zero mismatch counts.  It does not derive this
  table from an application of the composed map to the tetrahedra.
- `scripts/compose_t73_psi_A.py:37-42` raises a runtime error if asked to
  evaluate a generator containing `johnson_arm_restore`.  Lines 119--135
  accordingly record both `coordinate_point_evaluator` and
  `general_point_evaluator` as `OPEN`, while setting the topological status
  fields to `PASS`.
- `scripts/verify_t73_pl_homeomorphism.py` recomputes the 162-cell *section*
  restore and the linear transvections, but its `check_arm_restore` only
  compares the above counts, hashes and stored Boolean fields.  It therefore
  does not repair the missing global map.

The local layers may well be useful ingredients.  However, from the current
files one cannot determine the image of a general point, or even identify a
single common triangulation on which the alleged seven-layer composite is
piecewise affine.  Hence cell coverage, face compatibility, global
injectivity/surjectivity, periodic gluing, preservation of both handlebody
subcomplexes, and identity on (B) are not proved.

This is the earliest blocker because every later candidate-specific object
uses (\psi_A(C_i)), transported disk systems, or the induced boundary map.
Homology equality (\psi_{A*}=A) cannot substitute for the missing map.

### Data required to close this blocker

Either of the following would suffice:

- a flattened common subdivision with a complete source-to-image simplex
  table and reverse table, plus a verifier for the four items above; or
- an exact executable evaluator for each named layer, with formally proved
  domains, boundary identities and inverse laws, together with a composition
  verifier that computes the preimage/image of every Heegaard simplex and all
  quotient faces.

In addition, the resulting map must be joined to a relative isotopy from a
straightened linear representative of (A), or a theorem must be proved that
the chosen fixed-section surgery depends only on the supplied relative
mapping class and framing parity.  Equality on (H_1(T^3)) alone does not
establish this relative assertion.

## 4. The cancellation data do not yet define Kirby moves

Suppose the missing (\psi_A) had been supplied.  To cancel a (1/2)-pair one
must exhibit the attaching circle as an embedded framed circle in the boundary
of the current (0/1)-handlebody, meeting the belt sphere transversely in one
point, with the cancelling framing.  Before cancelling, every other attaching
component that runs through that one-handle must be slid over the cancelling
two-handle along a band embedded in the *current* boundary and disjoint from
the current full link except at its prescribed ends.  The pushed framing has
to be computed from the actual framing annuli.

The current cancellation programs do less:

- The small schema in `build_t73_p0_reconstruction_input.py:92-147` creates
  two unrelated coordinate cartoons in `R^3`.  Its `owner_transport` and
  `normal_field_transport` are literal `PASS` assignments.
- The newer `build_t73_belt_spheres.py:110-132` calls its segment/sphere test
  “coarse” and uses 21 sample points.  More importantly, the final (t)-pair
  intersection count is obtained from a preassembled passage list, while the
  (x)-pair count is the number of selected vertices on a lifted bottom arc;
  no common triangulated boundary containing belt spheres and every component
  is built.
- `verify_t73_handle_cancellation.py:240-280` checks that each band core stays
  on a coordinate belt face, has a distinct target, and carries the stored
  integer `relative_twist = 0`.  It never tests the band interior against the
  current full attaching link, never checks pairwise scheduling after earlier
  slides change the link, and never derives the relative twist from the two
  boundary normal fields.
- For the 1513 bands, `choose_bend` only prevents a finite set of *obstacle
  points* from lying on either segment.  It does not test intersection with
  obstacle edges/curves or with the evolving framed link.

Therefore the records prove word substitutions, passage counts and certain
coordinate incidences.  They do not meet the geometric hypotheses of the
Kirby cancellation theorem.  A vanishing terminal linking number, such as
(\operatorname{lk}(m_2,r_{yz})=0), is neither an embedded-band certificate
nor a computation of all transported framings.

### Data required to close the cancellation step

For each of the 6 and 1513 moves, supply the current link before the move, a
triangulated embedded rectangle (I\times I) in the same boundary, its two
attaching subintervals, proof that its interior misses the entire current
link, and the pushed normal field.  Recompute the next complete framed link
from that rectangle.  After all slides, verify exact transverse intersection
one and the cancelling relative framing for the two pairs.

## 5. The detector collar is not yet an embedded subset of the same boundary

`build_t73_actual_cut_tangle.py` derives 42+2 passage labels and then assigns
each one a vertical interval in a newly declared cube.  It directly sets
`pairwise_disjoint_height_monotone_arcs`, `disjoint_from_section_ball`, and
the final status to true.  The 227 remaining events are replaced by small
coordinate meridians with a prose `source_standardization`; no ambient
isotopy from the post-cancellation link is constructed.

The older P0 reconstruction verifier is explicit about the weakness.  It
checks that the twelve triangles have Euler characteristic two and then
accepts the solid as a ball only if the input field
`certified_topological_type` already says `3-ball`
(`reconstruct_t73_p0.py:89-116`).  It verifies strand monotonicity, but accepts
pairwise disjointness, the normal field, and the AR binding from stored
status fields (`:132-176`).  Thus the 44-strand braid is a meaningful finite
calculation in an abstract ball, but the inclusion of that ball and its
framed strands into the actual post-cancellation boundary is absent.

Closing P0 therefore also requires an explicit chart embedding
(B\hookrightarrow\partial W_1) after the two genuine Kirby movies, with
preimages of all 44 link arcs, all 227 complementary circles, and their
framing annuli.

## 6. A correct conditional E13 theorem

The following is the paper argument that would be valid once the missing
objects are provided.

### Proposition 2 (conditional P0/E13 implication)

Assume:

1. (\psi_A) is a splitting-preserving orientation-preserving PL
   homeomorphism of (T^3), pointwise fixed on a section ball, supplied with a
   relative isotopy to a straightened representative of the linear map (A);
2. the AR construction applied to (\psi_A) is realized by a complete
   embedded framed handle presentation, with the section-surgery handle in
   parity (\varepsilon=0);
3. the two proposed (1/2)-handle cancellations are genuine framed Kirby
   moves transporting the whole attaching link and the detector chart;
4. the three proposed 3-handle spheres are the actual outgoing attaching
   spheres in (\partial W_2), and simultaneous surgery on them gives an
   explicitly identified (S^3);
5. a homeomorphism (\partial D^4\to\partial W_3) is specified (or the upper
   handlebody is identified using Laudenbach--Poenaru after its hypotheses
   have been checked).

Then the closed manifold (X_J) obtained from this handle picture is
diffeomorphic to (\Sigma_A^0).

#### Proof

The relative isotopy in (1) induces a diffeomorphism of mapping tori carrying
the section neighborhood and its product framing to those for the
straightened linear representative.  Hence surgery gives
(\Sigma_A^0).  By AR, (2) is a handle decomposition of that surgery.
Genuine handle slides and cancellations in (3) do not change the
diffeomorphism type and transport every remaining labelled attaching datum.
Assumption (4) identifies the boundary after the three-handles with (S^3).
Attaching the (4)-handle by (5) completes the same closed manifold.  If one
changes only the attaching identification of the complete upper
(3/4)-handlebody, Laudenbach--Poenaru applies because its boundary is first
proved to be the required connected sum of (S^1\times S^2)'s; the boundary
diffeomorphism extends over the upper 1-handlebody.  Therefore the completion
is unique up to diffeomorphism, and (X_J\cong\Sigma_A^0).  □

The proposition isolates exactly what a valid paper proof must establish.
The determinant computations are not among its geometric hypotheses: they
prove that the already-defined standard surgery object is a homotopy sphere,
not that the candidate diagram is that object.

## 7. Why current P3/E13 do not satisfy Proposition 2

The file `geometry/t73_actual_W2_boundary.json` is not a triangulation or even
a cell decomposition of (\partial W_2).  It contains handle counts, hashes,
and strings declaring its boundary to be
(\#^3(S^1\times S^2)).  Its builder,
`build_t73_actual_sphere_system.py:111-151`, directly assigns:

- the boundary homeomorphism type;
- that the belt spheres are the actual attaching spheres;
- `identified_with_partial_W2 = True`;
- pairwise disjointness and connected complement;
- simultaneous surgery equal to (S^3).

The verifier at `verify_t73_actual_sphere_system.py:30-47` reads precisely
those Booleans back and rejects the file if they are false.  It does not
recognize a constructed boundary or perform the sphere surgeries.

P3 then makes a mathematically invalid inference.  At
`certify_t73_p3_four_handle.py:172-207`, an intersection-one dual loop is
converted into `cancels: True` for a purported (1/3)-handle cancellation.
Handles cancel only in consecutive indices.  A belt sphere of a reversed
1-handle is a useful *boundary-dual description* of a 3-handle attachment;
it is not an index-(1)/index-(3) cancellation in the same four-dimensional
handle decomposition.  Indeed, one handle in each of indices (0,1,3,4) is
the familiar handle decomposition of (S^1\times S^3), not (S^4).

At lines 233--256 the script then declares the remaining boundary to be
(S^3) and calls the boundary of an unrelated abstract (I^4) its attaching
map.  It supplies no map from (\partial I^4) to an actual
(\partial W_3).  The script's own header (`:12-14`) correctly says that it
is not a triangulation of (W_3) and does not identify (X_J) with
(\Sigma_A^0).

E13 does not add the missing geometry:

- `certify_t73_e13_close.py:501-585` creates ten prose pipeline records and
  assigns every one `status: PASS`.
- Stages 7--9 introduce two “railroad 1--3 cancellations” and then three
  “extra 1--3 pairs”.  No handle addition theorem or Kirby move relates this
  new handle inventory to the CS handle decomposition.  In fact the stored
  counts explicitly change from two surviving CS 1-handles to three new S
  1-handles.
- Lines 589--644 create a `resolved_maps` list by assertion and define success
  by ignoring the sole item still marked open.
- `certify_t73_e13_identification.py:346-380` then directly sets both
  `identified_with_X_J` and `identified_with_Sigma_A_0` to true.  No map of
  manifolds or Kirby sequence is constructed.

The live output exposes the contradiction in scope:

```text
P3: E13=PARTIAL, IDENTIFIED_WITH_SIGMA=False
E13 wrapper: E13=PASS, IDENTIFIED_WITH_SIGMA=True, MISSING_MAPS=1
```

These outputs are consistent as program states, but they are not a proof of
Proposition 2.

## 8. Minimal completion package

P0/E13 can be closed without formalizing all topology in Lean, but the paper
or its machine-checkable supplement must contain the following mathematical
objects:

1. a complete global map/inverse certificate for all 93 relative Johnson
   factors, with a common periodic triangulation and exact Heegaard/ball
   subcomplex images;
2. a relative-isotopy or relative-mapping-class theorem connecting that map
   to the chosen standard CS section surgery;
3. an embedded seven-component AR framed link in the boundary of one explicit
   (0/1)-handlebody;
4. 1519 sequential framed band embeddings (plus the six first-stage bands),
   each checked against the *current* whole link;
5. an explicit inclusion of the detector ball and its 44+227 cut pieces into
   the resulting boundary;
6. a cellulated (partial W_2), the three embedded attaching spheres inside
   it, and the result of simultaneous surgery, including an explicit
   homeomorphism to (S^3);
7. the attaching homeomorphism for (D^4), or a precise application of
   Laudenbach--Poenaru after the boundary hypotheses are independently proved;
8. the composite ambient equivalence tracking every labelled component and
   framing from the standard AR presentation through the moves to (X_J).

Until at least item 1 is supplied, no further paper exposition can turn the
present hierarchy into P0.  Until items 1--8 are supplied, the final statement
(X_J\cong\Sigma_A^0) remains an assumption.

## 10. Fail-closed ArmRestore gate

`audit/t73_canonical_arm_restore_schema.json` and
`scripts/verify_t73_canonical_arm_restore.py` now specify an admissible finite
replacement for the declaration-only restore assembly. Each layer must give
one common tetrahedral triangulation, exact source and target vertex
coordinates, the complete fixed boundary, owner labels, protected-cube data,
state hashes, and separating hyperplanes for non-face-adjacent pairs.

Using exact rational arithmetic, the verifier checks nondegeneracy and
orientation, face multiplicities, opposite-side adjacency, pairwise interior
separation, full chart volume, fixed boundary, owner preservation,
protected-cube avoidance and state chaining. These predicates make the vertex
map a fixed-boundary PL homeomorphism of the chart; certified successive
layers can then be composed by the elementary composition theorem.

The current `geometry/t73_johnson_restore_assembly.json` is deliberately not
accepted: it contains no common source/target vertex arrays or tetrahedral
map. The default gate returns `OPEN`. This defines a concrete target for a
future flattening generator, but does not fill item 1 of the minimal completion
package.

## 11. Paper-level rescue of the local paired saddle

The missing flattened affine cells are not necessary for the *existence* of
the local paired-saddle homeomorphism. The new verifier
`scripts/verify_t73_paired_saddle_topology.py` reconstructs each of the four
supports in the ambient period-four triangulation and checks every vertex
link. The respective supports have 85, 83, 122 and 122 tetrahedra; all
boundary-vertex links are disks, all interior-vertex links are spheres, and
each boundary is a connected triangulated sphere. The recorded elementary
collapse to a point is replayed. The source and target patches are connected
properly embedded triangulated disks: their boundary edges lie on the support
sphere, their remaining vertices and triangles lie in the interior. Finally,
the 5, 5, 40 and 40 recorded boundary triangle moves are replayed; every
intermediate curve is simple and the last curve is exactly the target-disk
boundary.

It follows by standard three-dimensional PL topology that each support is a
PL 3-ball. One proof doubles the contractible PL 3-manifold along its sphere
boundary, applies the three-dimensional Poincare theorem and PL Schoenflies,
and takes either complementary component. The boundary curve isotopy extends
over a collar. Its terminal extension carries the source disk to a proper
disk with the target boundary. Any two proper PL disks in a 3-ball with the
same boundary are ambient isotopic relative to that boundary: cap either disk
with the same boundary disk and use PL Schoenflies. Composing these maps sends
the source patch to the target patch. Taper the boundary isotopy to the
identity across a sufficiently thin *external* collar of the support sphere;
the resulting homeomorphism is fixed on the outer boundary of the enlarged
ball. Positive recorded clearance lets this collar be chosen disjoint from
the protected section ball.

Thus the disk-uniqueness/Alexander-trick route closes the existence of a local
paired-saddle ambient PL homeomorphism, and it proves that this local map can
be chosen isotopic to the identity and to carry the two owner sides in the
prescribed order. It does not construct a canonical coordinate evaluator and
does not identify the arbitrary homeomorphism supplied by the theorem with
the later stored spine-transport or detector polylines. Consequently this
repairs one local ArmRestore existence step but does not close the global P0
or E13 chain.
