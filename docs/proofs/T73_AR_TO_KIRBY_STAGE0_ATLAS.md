# Stage-0 atlas for the AR-to-Kirby presentation

## Outcome

The current repository determines a checked **source atlas**, but it does not
determine a common dotted-circle Kirby presentation. The source atlas is
saved as `geometry/t73_ar_source_coordinate_atlas.json`, generated by
`scripts/build_t73_ar_source_coordinate_atlas.py`, described by
`data/T73_AR_SOURCE_COORDINATE_ATLAS.schema.json`, and checked by
`scripts/verify_t73_ar_source_coordinate_atlas.py`.

This distinction prevents two invalid shortcuts:

1. the fourth coordinate of a mapping-torus point is $u$, while the fourth
   coordinate of the cubical $x$-belt chart is the local normal coordinate
   $ν$; and
2. the attaching boundary $#^g(S¹ × S²)$ does not embed globally in $S³$.
   A dotted-circle picture is a surgery presentation obtained after cutting
   along belt spheres, not an ambient embedding of that boundary.

## Verified source charts

The atlas binds the live SHA values of the actual AR link, both belt-sphere
systems, both cancellation schedules, and the triangulated Johnson/AR fiber
handlebody pair.

| chart | coordinates | verified objects | limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| `mapping_torus_T3xI` | $(x,y,z,u)$ | $m_1,m_2,m_3,h_{CS}$ | seam is $(p,1)∼(ψ_A(p),0)$ |
| `fiber_dual_H2_T3` | $(x,y,z)$ | $r_{xy},r_{yz},r_{zx}$ | no $u$-level or side is stored |
| `t_belt_slice` | $(x,y,z)↦(x,y,z,1/2)$ | belt and six band centers | full band edges are absent |
| `x_belt_local` | $(y,z,ν)$, locally $(2,y,z,ν)$ | belt and 1513 positive-face band centers | no collar map from $ν$ to the mapping-torus/cut-handlebody normal |
| `johnson_ar_fiber_pair` | triangulated 3-dimensional fiber | two genus-three handlebodies | not a mapping-torus or Kirby-presentation chart |

Every one of the seven pre-cancellation cores is dereferenced from
`geometry/t73_actual_ar_link.json`, checked for closure, and bound by its own
SHA. The exact vertex counts are

$$
|m_1|=23,\quad |m_2|=1877,\quad |m_3|=8783,\quad |h_{CS}|=2,\quad
|r_{xy}|=|r_{yz}|=|r_{zx}|=9.
$$

For $m_1,m_2,m_3$, the procedural product annuli and their relative twist
zero are also hash-bound. The section handle has its procedural annulus. For
the three dual-cell components, the dual disks are present but no closed
push-off polyline or ribbon vertex/triangle list is stored; the atlas keeps
those three framings `OPEN`.

This exposes a data-completeness distinction hidden by the upstream
`actual_framed_ar_link = PASS` field: that status records the mathematical
product-framing prescription, but the serialized object contains zero
explicit dual-cell ribbons and zero dotted-meridian cores. The atlas does not
reinterpret a named framing as the missing coordinates.

## Why coordinatewise gluing is invalid

The $x$-belt builder explicitly declares

```text
x=2, boundary of [-1,1]_(y,z,normal)^3.
```

Its vertices have $ν=-1$ and $ν=1$, whereas the mapping-torus coordinate has
$u∈[0,1]$. Thus the tempting identification

$$
(2,y,z,ν)↦(2,y,z,u=ν)
$$

does not even land in the stored mapping-torus interval for half of the belt.
A valid overlap needs at least a collar choice $u=u_0+ε ν$, together with a
choice of seam side and the action of $ψ_A$ if that collar crosses the seam.
Neither $u_0$, $ε$, nor the seam-side transition is stored.

Independently, the dual-cell polylines contain only three coordinates. Their
membership in the fiber $H_2$ is known, but their $u$-level, side collar, and
ribbon normal in the mapping-torus boundary are not fields of the artifact.
This is the first missing transition in the stored coordinate chain.

## Why the target must be a presentation

Before cancellation the handle counts give four 1-handles and seven
2-handles. A complete dotted-circle snapshot must therefore contain

```text
dotted x,y,z,t + cores m_1,m_2,m_3,h_CS,r_xy,r_yz,r_zx.
```

After cancelling $(t,h_{CS})$ and $(x,m_1)$, the complete snapshot contains

```text
dotted y,z + cores m_2,m_3,r_xy,r_yz,r_zx.
```

Consequently “seven pre-cancellation cores plus two dotted meridians” mixes
two different movie times and is not a complete Kirby diagram at either
time.

There is also a formal obstruction to interpreting the requested map as an
ambient embedding. If a closed connected 3-manifold embedded in connected
$S³$, invariance of domain would make its image open; compactness makes the
image closed. It would therefore be all of $S³$. But

$$
H_1(#^g(S¹ × S²);ℤ)=ℤ^g≠0=H_1(S³;ℤ)
$$

for $g=4$ and $g=2$. The missing object must instead be either:

* a cut-and-surgery presentation with dotted circles, foot disks, quotient
  pairings and cut-open curve/ribbon pieces; or
* an explicit triangulation of $#^g(S¹ × S²)$ with the curves and ribbons
  embedded as marked subcomplexes.

## Exact missing choices

In dependency order, a complete construction still needs:

1. `fiber_dual_H2_to_mapping_torus`: the $u$-level/side and compatible seam
   collar for all three dual-cell cores and their ribbons;
2. `x_belt_local_to_mapping_torus_or_cut_handlebody`: the $u_0,ε$ collar and
   seam-side transition for the $x$-belt normal coordinate;
3. `cut_handlebody_to_dotted_S3_presentation`: four pre-cancellation handle
   foot pairs, orientation-reversing disk identifications, every lane endpoint
   map, and ribbon-normal trivializations;
4. `full_band_rectangles_and_surgery_splices`: both boundary edges and exact
   attachment parameters for six $t$-bands and 1513 $x$-bands, plus every
   embedded parallel replacement arc and final cyclic splice.

The stored band centers and event schedules constrain these choices but do
not determine them. Choosing them ad hoc would create a new Kirby diagram
without proving it represents the saved AR geometry.
