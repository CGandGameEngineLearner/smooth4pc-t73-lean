## Result

The six-sweep data define a genuine abstract 44-strand braid in a standard
punctured disk.  They do not define an embedding of that disk into the
boundary of the actual Aitchison--Rubinstein two-handle presentation.  The
compact Kirby ledger defines free-group words and names a product normal; it
does not define the simultaneous embedded framed attaching link.

Accordingly P0 remains **OPEN**.  This attempt identifies the first
irrecoverable datum and replaces the vague request for a large planar file by
a smaller machine-readable witness specification.

matching the historical ledger.  Thus the AR page-specific construction can

Laudenbach--Poenaru, *A note on 4-dimensional handlebodies*,
[Numdam scan](https://www.numdam.org/item/BSMF_1972__100__337_0/), was checked
directly.  Its theorem controls the gluing/attachment ambiguity for the
standard four-dimensional one-/three-handlebodies.  It may transport an
already specified whole framed link under a boundary diffeomorphism.  It does
not assert that two links representing the same conjugacy classes in the free
group are related by such a diffeomorphism, and it does not manufacture the
missing whole-link map.

## Non-reconstructability lemma

**Lemma.** A list of free-group words, component labels, and integer framings
does not determine a framed attaching link in the boundary of a
four-dimensional one-handlebody.

**Proof.** Start with any component and choose a small 3-ball meeting it in an
unknotted arc, disjoint from the one-handle belt disks and all other
components.  Replace the arc by its connected sum with a nontrivial knot,
for example a trefoil, and extend the chosen framing with zero additional
twist.  This local knotting leaves every intersection with the belt disks,
hence every free-group word, unchanged; it also leaves the recorded integer
framing unchanged.  The framed links need not be isotopic.  In the special
case of the empty word in `S^3`, zero-framed surgery on the unknot has boundary
`S^1 x S^2`, while zero-framed surgery on a trefoil has a different surgery
manifold.  Thus the corresponding two-handlebodies are not determined by the
word/framing ledger.  The same local operation works in a ball in
`#^g(S^1 x S^2)`.  QED.

This lemma does not say that the proposed AR embedding is wrong.  It proves
that the public compact ledger cannot identify it without additional
embedded data.

## Minimal public P0 witness

The schema `audit/t73_p0_embedded_witness_schema.json` requires four layers.

1. **Actual framed link.** Parametric PL embeddings of every component in a
   fixed triangulated model of `partial W_1`, pairwise-disjointness data, and
   a nonzero normal field/framing along each component.
2. **AR provenance.** A construction from the displayed matrix and the
   chosen straightening, together with a checked map from the AR product
   annuli to the parametric link.  Merely reproducing relator words is
   rejected.
3. **Cancellations.** Local ambient-isotopy/handle-cancellation patches for
   `(t,h_CS)` and `(x,m_1)`, with all components, owner labels and normal
   fields transported through every patch.
4. **Detector collar.** A parametric embedding of the standard punctured
   disk/cylinder whose inverse image of the attaching link is exactly the 44
   labelled wickets, and whose induced point-push word is the pinned public
   braid.

An eventual witness may use a compact PL/triangulated representation rather
than the historical two-million-crossing projection.  What cannot be omitted
is the embedding and simultaneous framing/label transport.

## Next dependency

Once this P0 witness exists, it canonically determines the actual product
motion `B_act` and the endpoint coordinate transport `P`.  Those are the first
inputs to the coefficient-bimodule action squares in C.  Constructing C before
this P0 collar is fixed would repeat the circular coordinate choice rejected
by the adversarial audit.
